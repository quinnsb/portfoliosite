An attempt to install a functional pneumatic tube mail delivery system between the home office and the kitchen. The system is designed to transport small notes, snacks, and occasionally a pen that someone left in the other room.

## 2026.05.02

Tube diameter increased to 4 inches after the pretzel incident. System now accommodates most standard office supplies and a limited selection of granola bars. Throughput improved but noise levels remain problematic during video calls.

## 2026.01.18

First successful delivery: a Post-It note reading "SEND COFFEE" traveled 14 meters in 2.3 seconds. The note arrived slightly crumpled but legible. The coffee did not follow.

## 2025.11.03

Project initiated after the realization that walking to the kitchen takes approximately 45 seconds — an eternity when measured in units of productive thought lost. Initial feasibility study suggests PVC pipe and a shop vacuum as the propulsion mechanism.
