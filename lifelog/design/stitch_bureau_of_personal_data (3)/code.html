On the Physics of Melancholy

The quantification of abstract emotional states has long evaded rigorous institutional analysis. However, recent observations in sector 4 have isolated a peculiar energetic signature associated with prolonged periods of inertia. We hypothesize that melancholy is not merely a psychological condition, but a localized distortion of physical space, measurable in units of micro-drag.

When a subject enters a state of profound apathy, the surrounding atmosphere demonstrably thickens. Air resistance increases by a factor of 1.04, and photons experience a slight delay traversing the immediate vicinity. This is the weight of sorrow made literal.

II. Mechanics of Sorrow

To understand this friction, we must look at the standard model of localized atmospheric pressure. The standard ambient emotional baseline operates at exactly 1.0 ATM. Variations previously thought to be negligible now reveal significant thermal fluctuations.

"The subject reported feeling 'heavy,' a sensation corroborated by gravimetric sensors indicating a 0.02% localized mass increase."
— LOG ENTRY: DR. A. VANCE, CYCLE 44

This indicates that the subjective experience of carrying a 'burden' translates into an objective, albeit minute, gravitational anomaly.

III. Density Mass

The formulation for calculating this density shift is documented below. It is imperative to note the inverse relationship between cognitive velocity and atmospheric drag. 

[ CALCULATION PROTOCOL // OMEGA-9 ]
M_melancholy = (T_inertia * D_atmospheric) / (V_cognitive + ε)

WHERE:
T = Time observed in stasis
D = Baseline atmospheric density
V = Speed of internal monologue processing
ε = Constant of systemic fatigue (0.0034)

As cognitive velocity approaches zero, the melancholy mass approaches infinity, theoretically creating a microscopic singularity of despair. Protocols are in place to artificially stimulate cognitive velocity should this threshold be neared.
