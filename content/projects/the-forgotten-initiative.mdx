A visual body of work from three years helping The Forgotten Initiative communicate foster care with clarity, warmth, and momentum.

## What I Did

- Creative direction and graphic design
- Podcast production and show launch support
- Video editing, motion graphics, and interview production
- Blog writing, fundraising content, and resource development
- Communications leadership across campaigns, channels, and brand touchpoints

## Context

I started as a creative content producer in 2021 and later stepped into communications director. The work ranged from daily design needs to larger strategic projects, including The Forgotten Podcast, Just Neighbors, and Foster Care in the Church, a free education resource for churches that deserves its own deeper case study.
